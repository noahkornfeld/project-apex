2

grep reward_components or apex_run.log for cost_t values in Fold 5's OOS window around 2019-11-25. If a single week shows cost_t > 0.10 (10% of NAV), the holiday discount is not adequately suppressing large trades in thin markets. Consider a hard per-step trade-size cap during holiday windows. Also review whether the metrics.py cost_drag formula should use gross P&L (before costs) rather than net P&L in the denominator.

3
Check model_ep2.pt checkpoint for Fold 3 before attributing MaxDD solely to clip

Beta rose 0.96 → 1.24. If beta is lower at the end of Ep2 vs Ep3, the MaxDD regression is an Ep3-specific overconfidence effect that the clip change amplified. Compare OOS metrics using the Ep2 checkpoint vs the Ep3 (final) checkpoint for Fold 3 — this would confirm whether reverting the clip fully recovers performance or whether deeper changes are needed.

4
Consider reducing HOLIDAY_PRE_DAYS from 14 to 7

The 14-day pre-holiday window may be too wide: it creates two-week periods where the agent holds concentrated positions it cannot cheaply rebalance, which then produce extreme return spikes when full volume returns. A 7-day window (one trading week before the holiday) would still capture the thin-market environment without trapping the agent in prolonged illiquid exposure. Evaluate impact on Fold 2 (Sep 2013) and Fold 4 (Jan 2017) specifically before applying broadly.
