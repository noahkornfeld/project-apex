Section 7 — Observations & Recommendations
What went well

Run 6 is the strongest run to date on headline metrics. Avg Sharpe 1.259 improves on Run 4 (1.109) and Run 5 (1.180), and beat rate 6/8 is the best across all runs. All three targeted fixes delivered: clip=30 directly fixed Fold 3 MaxDD (−37.3%→−19.6%) and Fold 8 Sharpe (0.824→1.191) while holding Fold 7 well above Run 4 (0.971 vs 0.342). The HOLIDAY_PRE_DAYS reduction eliminated the extreme kurtosis spike in Fold 2 (>50→13.5). The new cost_drag formula produces stable, interpretable values across all folds (0.04%–0.72% in 7/8 folds). Folds 3 and 4 delivered their best-ever results across all runs, and Fold 4 Sharpe 2.055 is a new all-runs record. Avg Excess CAGR of +20.33% is also the best across all runs.

What regressed

Fold 1 is the sole serious regression — a stochastic Q-divergence in Ep3 at update ~15000 (|q_mean|=107) that did not self-correct, freezing the policy and producing Sharpe −0.113 and MaxDD −51.6%. This failure is seed-dependent and not attributable to the three code changes (Run 4 and Run 5 both produced Sharpe 0.81 and 1.24 respectively for this fold under similar conditions). Holiday spikes persist in Folds 4, 7, and 8 despite the HOLIDAY_PRE_DAYS reduction — the mechanism (pre-built concentrated positions entering thin-market weeks) cannot be addressed by the ADV discount window alone. Fold 8 hit rate (43.4%) remains a structural concern for the AI-concentration era. Fold 2 regressed relative to Run 4 (−0.279 Sharpe delta), though the absolute value (1.537) remains strong; the regression may reflect reduced holiday-position exploitation after the HOLIDAY_PRE_DAYS change.

Run 7 recommendations

The highest-priority change for Run 7 is per-episode checkpoint selection: after each fold's training completes, evaluate OOS performance for model_ep1.pt, model_ep2.pt, and model_ep3.pt and retain the best-performing checkpoint. This would rescue folds like Fold 1 where Ep3 diverges while Ep2 converges cleanly. Second priority: run Fold 1 alone (−−fold 1) to determine if the Q-divergence failure is seed-dependent or structural to the 2005–2009 training window; if it fails again, investigate replay buffer composition. No hyperparameter changes are recommended: clip=30 is confirmed correct, lambda_slow=0.50 is working, and the cost_drag fix should be retained. Consider a max single-position-size cap (e.g., 20%) applied only during OOS evaluation to reduce kurtosis in Folds 4, 7, and 8 without touching training policy. Run 6 is confirmed as the new baseline; all three code changes carry forward to Run 7.
