Project APEX

Logo Concepts — A Form + Reinforcement Learning Loop · Gold Colorway
Concept 01
The Integrated A
The A and the loop are one continuous stroke. The apex of the letter is replaced by the RL cycle — the loop literally is the peak. A small right-pointing chevron at the crest shows the direction of the feedback. Minimal and unified.
Concept 02
The Learning Arc
A traditional open-bottom A with a separate arc looping around the apex. The arc has a clear arrowhead showing the RL feedback direction and a chevron at the crest confirming clockwise rotation. Crossbar tick marks add data precision. The apex dot anchors the peak.
Concept 03
The Bold Mark
Heavier strokes with a radial glow behind the apex, outer orbital ring, and endpoint nodes on the loop. The data dots along the bottom reference the 110-name universe. The layered glow rings behind the apex give it a sense of energy and focus at the peak.
